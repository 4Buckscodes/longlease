LET’S TALK ABOUT THE GAP

There is property waiting to be used. And there are people looking for the right place to use.

Think about a completed building that has been vacant for months. The owner is carrying the cost, waiting for the right tenant. Somewhere else, a business is ready to expand but cannot justify buying an entire property just to get started. Both have something the other needs. The missing piece is often a clear route to a suitable long-term lease. That is the connection LONGLEASE is here to make.

HOW WE HELP YOU MOVE FORWARD

From “this could work” to a properly considered lease.

A good property match is more than a pin on a map. It starts with understanding what the owner is offering, what the occupier needs and whether the two can work commercially. Here is how we help move that conversation along.

01 / DISCOVER

Tell us what you have — or what you need

Owners share the property, its current condition and preferred lease terms. Occupiers tell us the location, space, budget and intended use they have in mind.

02 / EVALUATE

Have the real conversation

Discuss rent, lease length, repairs, fit-out, permitted use, responsibilities and approvals. If the numbers or conditions do not make sense, it is better to know early.

03 / INSPECT

Inspect. Verify. Put it in writing.

See the property, verify ownership and authority, carry out your checks and have a qualified professional review the agreement. Nothing is settled until the parties agree and sign the appropriate documents.

FOR OWNERS, DEVELOPERS & ASSET MANAGERS
