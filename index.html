Frequently asked questions

Common questions about LONGLEASE

Does LONGLEASE own the properties listed?

No. LONGLEASE is a connection point for property owners, authorised representatives and people seeking long-term access. Ownership and authority should always be independently verified.

Does LONGLEASE guarantee that I will find a tenant or a property?

No. We facilitate enquiries and introductions. A suitable match depends on the property, the intended use, the commercial terms and the parties involved.

Can I lease a property for a business or a different operating model?

Potentially, subject to the owner’s written consent, the property’s condition, title and permitted use, planning requirements and any sector-specific approvals.

Does LONGLEASE collect rent or provide escrow?

The current enquiry process does not collect rent or hold funds. Payment arrangements should be agreed and documented directly by the parties using appropriate professional advice.

What should I check before signing?

Confirm ownership and authority, inspect the property, establish permitted use, understand all costs and responsibilities, and have a qualified property or legal professional review the lease.
