THE ECONOMICS OF REAL ESTATE ACCESS

Property is an asset. Its potential is realised when it is put to use.

Across real estate, ownership and utilisation are not always the same thing. Properties can remain vacant or underused while businesses and operators struggle to secure suitable space without committing capital to acquisition. LONGLEASE brings these sides of the market together through a marketplace for long-term leasing — helping owners explore productive use of their assets and helping occupiers access property for living, business and operations.

RESIDENTIAL PROPERTY

Access homes and apartments for long-term occupation, with lease terms discussed directly with the relevant owner or representative.

COMMERCIAL & INSTITUTIONAL SPACE

Explore premises for offices, retail, education, healthcare, hospitality and other commercial or institutional purposes, subject to permitted use.

PROPERTY FOR BUSINESS OPERATIONS

Explore properties that may support serviced apartments, hostels, student accommodation, co-living and other operating models, subject to suitability, approvals and the owner’s written consent.

READY-TO-OCCUPY ASSETS

Identify completed properties that may be available for occupation or operation and assess their condition, terms and suitability before commitment.

DEVELOPMENT & OFF-PLAN

Explore selected developments and assess proposed delivery timelines, intended use and the commercial terms available.

STRATEGIC PROPERTY OPPORTUNITIES

Consider opportunities involving property access, leasing and utilisation. Assess each proposition on its own merits, costs, risks and contractual terms.

FROM PROPERTY ACCESS TO PRODUCTIVE USE

Connect the asset with its next use.

LONGLEASE provides a starting point for owners, businesses and operators to explore a potential leasing relationship. Every opportunity should be assessed through due diligence, clear terms and a properly documented agreement.

01 / DISCOVER

Define what you need

Owners outline the property and preferred leasing terms. Businesses and operators identify the location, space and permitted use they require.

02 / EVALUATE

Assess the opportunity

Review suitability, condition, lease duration, rent, responsibilities, approvals and any operational requirements before making a commitment.

03 / INSPECT

Agree the terms

Arrange an inspection, complete independent checks and document the agreed terms directly with the relevant parties. An enquiry through LONGLEASE does not itself create a lease or guarantee an outcome.
