THE OPPORTUNITY

One side has space. The other has a plan.

A vacant building costs its owner money. A growing business needs room without buying the building. LONGLEASE brings both sides to the same table through long-term leasing.

A PLACE TO CALL HOME

Looking for a home or apartment for the longer term? Explore residential spaces and discuss the rent, duration and conditions with the owner or authorised representative.

ROOM TO DO BUSINESS

From an office or shop to a school, clinic or hospitality space, find premises that fit the way you work. We help start the conversation; permitted use and approvals must be confirmed.

SPACE TO BUILD AN OPERATION

Have a plan for serviced apartments, student accommodation, a hostel or co-living? Explore properties that may suit the model, with the owner’s written consent and all required approvals in place.

COMPLETED AND READY

Prefer a property you can walk through today? Explore completed spaces, arrange a viewing and ask the practical questions before making a decision.

LOOKING AHEAD

Explore selected developments and off-plan opportunities. Understand what is being offered, when delivery is expected and what conditions apply before you commit.

OPPORTUNITIES WORTH EXPLORING

Some properties call for a more considered conversation about location, lease structure, fit-out, operating costs and potential use. Start that conversation here — then do your due diligence.

A CLEARER ROUTE TO A LEASE

Three steps. No guesswork.

A straightforward process for owners and occupiers to see whether the property, the plans and the terms fit.

01 / DISCOVER

Share the brief

Owners provide property details and preferred terms. Occupiers share their location, space, budget and intended use.

02 / EVALUATE

Agree the key terms

Discuss rent, lease length, repairs, fit-out, permitted use and responsibilities. Establish whether the arrangement makes commercial sense.

03 / INSPECT

Inspect and verify

View the property, confirm ownership and authority, complete your checks and have the agreement professionally reviewed before signing.
